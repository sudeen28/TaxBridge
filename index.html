Location matters — sometimes

Local when it matters. Remote when it doesn't.

For engagements that need someone on the ground, we prioritize firms based in or near your location. For work that can be handled remotely, geography matters a lot less — and that's factored into which firms we suggest.

On-site workInvestigations, audits with physical records, and matters requiring an in-person presence get matched to firms who can actually show up — starting with firms in your city or state.
Remote-friendly workBookkeeping, filings, and advisory that don't need anyone on-site open up to well-suited firms further afield, so you're not limited to who happens to be local.
